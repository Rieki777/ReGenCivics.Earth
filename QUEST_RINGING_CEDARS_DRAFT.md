### Story Card

Somewhere in the Siberian wilderness, one family preserved an unbroken thread of knowledge about how humans once lived in genuine harmony with the Earth. Anastasia, the woman at the centre of this series, did not inherit a museum piece. She inherited a living practice. The Ringing Cedars books carry that practice out into the world in story form, and what has happened since is hard to explain until you read them: millions of people across Russia and the Baltics and the world changed how they live. Hundreds of settlements formed. Gardens appeared. Communities reorganized themselves around the question of what it means to grow a child, and a culture, in full relationship with the land.

These books are not a fundamentalist blueprint or a new religion. They are a remembering. Take what inspires, leave what doesn't - but go in with your heart open, because something in this series has a way of staying with you long after you put it down.

### How To Do This Quest

**Step 1: Get the books.** The English translations are hard to find in print - the translations went out of print and they are revered enough that scarcity is real. The quest author has compiled all 10 books as free PDFs here: https://icedrive.net/s/PxAyCa5kSGaT4224Yw7i63B1hBhB. The official U.S. based website for the foundation is anastasia.foundation. For audio: use a PDF-to-voice reader (Natural Reader, Speechify, or your phone's built-in read-aloud) to turn any book into an audiobook while you work, walk, or garden.

**Step 2: Read (or listen) with intention.** Each book can stand alone, but the series builds. Book 1 introduces Anastasia and her world. By books 7-9, you are in different territory entirely. Rye's note: the last books are where it gets genuinely extraordinary. Keep going.

**Step 3: Read through a regenerative lens.** As you go, notice: How did her people organize their communities? How did they think about land, child-rearing, governance, food, relationship to nature, and the long arc of civilization? You don't have to agree with everything. You're mining for wisdom and inspiration that applies to what we're building now.

**Step 4: Post your reflection in the forum after each book.** Go to the Ringing Cedars thread in the forum. Write what sparked for you, what you're sitting with, what surprised you, and what you might carry into your own practice or community. Even a few paragraphs count. These reflections are the real deliverable and value add for our community - they build a living archive of how this wisdom is landing in people across the movement and how we can practically apply it today.

**Step 5: Optional social share.** After finishing each book, share a quote, reflection, or image on social media that captures something from it. Tag regencivics.earth if you're willing. This spreads the series to people who may never have heard of it.

**Step 6: Claim your tokens.** When you are done reading -- whether that's 1 book or all 10 -- claim your tokens. You earn 33 $ReGen + 1 RGVoice per book read. One claim only: once you claim, you cannot come back and add more books. Finish what you plan to read before claiming. Complete all 10 and you earn 333 $ReGen + 10 RGVoice, plus the cedar tree badge.

### Tips
- Don't be put off by Book 1 - it has a framing that some find unusual. The wisdom compounds across the series. Keep reading.
- The PDF-to-audio approach works beautifully for this series. Many people in the community listen while gardening, driving, cooking, or walking.
- The series sparked a literal settlement movement in Russia and the Baltics - which has only just now really begun to spread further. If you want to go deeper after reading, search for "Kin's Domain" or "Anastasia settlements" to see what people built from this inspiration.
- The series is originally in Russian by Vladimir Megre. If you read Russian or speak another language with a translation, seek that version - something is always slightly different in translation.
- You don't need to agree with everything Anastasia says. Read as you would any wisdom tradition: take what resonates, hold the rest lightly.
