# Season Details from Roadmap Page

## Winter - Assessment (Local - Yin - In - Consolidation)
**Theme:** Wisdom Work, Preparations and Administration
**Tagline:** Govern, Decide, Redesign, & Prepare our Projects for Spring

**Activities:**
- Making Decisions: After the Fall Season of reflection and grounding we are more prepared to make wise decisions for our projects, organizations, and communities
- Crafting and designing our organizations (and new DHO/DAO/Economic/Organization/Etc structures)
- Creating templates, making fliers, creating our various structures
- Project Economy and Organization structures built
- V1.0 Project Contributor Guides created
- V1.0 Economic and token-economic structures designed
- Legal structures established
- V1.0 Organisation structures established (circles, roles, etc)
- Initial foundational Policies established
- Prepared for crowdfunding (soft/hard caps, bonuses, etc)

**Note:** *Projects who have completed their own "Winter Season" are invited to apply for the "Spring Season Incubator"*

## Spring - Planting (Global - Yang - Out - Expansion)
**Theme:** Co-Creating Our Games, Together
**Tagline:** Launch new Crowd-Pooling Campaigns

**Activities:**
- Prepare and run crowd pooling campaigns for Regenerative Civics projects
- Communities pool a variety of the 9+ forms of capital needed to run villages, cooperative food systems, material libraries, or any socially or ecologically regenerative project
- Storytelling campaigns, alliance building, forming teams, pooling resources
- Preparing for the summer actions
- Each Spring Season we take a cohort of 13 land projects through an open-source journey, building all the foundations you need to accept capital (funding, time, etc) from other people!

## Summer - Growth (Global - Yang - Out - Expansion)
**Theme:** Evolving and Building our Projects
**Tagline:** Regen Civics Festivals - On-the-ground Regeneration

**Activities:**
- Regen civics raising festivals for in-person regenerative activations
- Physically coming together in community across the globe
- Doing the work of ecological and social regeneration
- Put our tools to the test to see how they help us with the real work of on-the-ground systems change
- Fresh air, celebration and festivals, sunlight, hands in the soil: build, repair, & regenerate
- Our Alliance Partners help establish physical foundations and the practical pieces for successful projects

## Fall/Autumn - Abundance (Local - Yin - In - Consolidation)
**Theme:** Enjoying our Abundance
**Tagline:** Regen Civics Life / Reflection / Harvest

**Activities:**
- ReGen Harvest Festivals where we share learnings, experience and wisdom to inform our Winter Seasons activities
- Family and Village wellbeing and thrivability - ensuring we're consciously giving active space to co-create with our families and local communities
- Personal wellbeing and thrivability - Intentionally leaving space for the incredibly important inner work and regenerating our inner landscapes
- Making Sense - Reflect on economic & governance protocols
- A time of reflection on how our Governance systems served us (or didn't) in serving our purpose the previous seasons
- Reflect, research, explore, & sense-make
- Rest, celebrate, enjoy, love, reflect and nourish ourselves as we prepare for another year growing the ReGenerative Renaissance
