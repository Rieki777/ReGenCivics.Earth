# Tripetto Form Structure Notes

## Main Routing Page

**Title:** "Hi Friend, welcome to the ReGenerative Renaissance! Which most apply to you?"

**Subtitle:** "If more than one apply, feel free to fill out this form multiple times."

**Description:** "This short form helps us know what types of opportunities to connect with you about, so all our communications are relevant to you."

## 7 Path Options:

1. **APPLY For Next Season / Land Partner** - I (co)steward/own land and would like to explore joining the Alliance
2. **Create with ReGens** - I want to co-create with one/some of the Alliance Organizations
3. **Alliance** - I represent an organization and I want to explore joining the Alliance!
4. **Finance the Renaissance** - I represent a Fund/Institution interested in Systemic ReGeneration and I want to explore funding the Alliance.
5. **Live** - I want to co-create with one/some of the ReGen Land Projects
6. **Role** - Apply for a role in ReGen Civics directly
7. **Something else?**

---

## Path Details (to be documented by exploring each path):


### Path 1: APPLY For Next Season / Land Partner

**Fields:**
1. Best link to explore your project? (URL input)
2. Tell us more about what inspired you to reach out! Feel free to also leave empty :) (text area, optional)
3. Where are you at with your project? (checkboxes)
   - Team of 3+ committed core members.
   - Land under a structure with team having 100% authority.
   - Vision, Mission, Purpose, Values, etc clearly defined.
   - Governance, legal and economic system designed.
   - 3+ Families living on the land.
   (more options likely below)


### Path 3: Alliance (Organization joining)

**Fields:**
1. Best link to explore your organization? (URL input)
2. Tell us more about how our partnership will help create a diversity of Regenerative Cultures! (text area)
3. Best email to reach you? (email input)


### Path 4: Finance the Renaissance (Investor)

**Fields:**
1. Best link to explore your fund or institution? (URL input)
2. Cash or Crypto? (checkboxes)
   - Cash
   - Crypto
   - Both
3. Transaction more than $1MM? (likely yes/no)
