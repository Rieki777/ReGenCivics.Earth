# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are building infrastructure for the Regenerative Renaissance. The work of healing communities, ecosystems, and economies happens in groups that respect each other. This project follows that.

We pledge to make participation in this community a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community
- Speaking with care about land, people, and the work we are doing together

Unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **rieki@pm.me**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy and security of the reporter is preserved.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (the GitHub repo, the forum at regencivics.earth/community, Discord, in-person gatherings) and in 1:1 interactions when an individual is officially representing the community.

## Enforcement Guidelines

| Behavior | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Inappropriate language or unprofessional conduct (first offense) | Private warning with explanation. Public apology may be requested. |
| Repeated inappropriate behavior or a single incident of harassment | Temporary ban from any interaction or public communication for a specified period. |
| Sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward classes of individuals | Permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community. |
