# LinkedIn Post: Organizations Designed to Meet Needs

By Rieki (Rye) Cordon

**Key Question:** What if organizations were actually designed to meet the needs of people?

Too often we hear the phrase "that doesn't belong at work" when human needs are being expressed in our org environments.

Most orgs are designed to meet the needs of an "economy" with the singular goal of providing a product/service for profit (often to the detriment of people).

**So, imagine this:**

An org starts when a group of humans come together with the express goal of meeting their needs together.

**For example:**
7 friends, who already love being and creating things together, decide they want to start a "village org(temp. name)"

It begins with them collectively identifying all their shared:
1. NEEDS
2. RESOURCES
3. POTENTIAL

**1. NEEDS**
Needs are the most simple, as objectively humans share a set of biological needs.
So we can start with this list and augment it to meet the specific nuances of each group.
Now they can visualize and identify what their actual needs are and what they're working to meet together.

**2. RESOURCES**
One tool here is the "8/9/10 forms of capital" frameworks, where financial capital (stocks, money, etc.) is simply 1 form.
Now they can visualize a much fuller extent of the resources they share together.

**3. POTENTIAL**
What are the unique skills, passions, and dreams each member brings?
