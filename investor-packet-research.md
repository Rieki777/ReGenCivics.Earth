# Investor Packet Research Notes

## Key Industry Insights from BCG Research

### Market Opportunity
- Regenerative landscapes represent a $310 billion global investment opportunity
- Expected IRR of 15-30% over 10-year periods for commercial investors
- Only 7% of climate finance currently goes toward sustainable food systems
- Yields for key commodities could decline 15-35% by 2050 without action

### Investment Structure Best Practices
1. **Blended Finance Approach** - Combines catalytic capital, direct farmer support, and commercial capital
2. **De-risking Mechanisms** - Subsidized lending, concessional debt, guarantees
3. **Direct Support** - Grants, payments for ecosystem services, technical assistance
4. **Commercial Capital** - Unlocked through proof points and risk reduction

### Key Success Factors
- Integrated financing structures that de-risk transitions
- Aligned policy frameworks
- Efficient measurement, reporting, and verification systems
- Place-based landscape programs
