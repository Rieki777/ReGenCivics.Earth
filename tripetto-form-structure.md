# Tripetto Form Structure - 7 Routing Paths

## Main Question
"Hi Friend, welcome to the ReGenerative Renaissance! Which most apply to you?"
- If more than one apply, feel free to fill out this form multiple times.
- This short form helps us know what types of opportunities to connect with you about, so all our communications are relevant to you.

## 7 Routing Paths

1. **APPLY For Next Season / Land Partner**
   - "I (co)steward/own land and would like to explore joining the Alliance"
   - Route: Land project application

2. **Create with ReGens**
   - "I want to co-create with one/some of the Alliance Organizations"
   - Route: Collaboration with alliance organizations

3. **Alliance**
   - "I represent an organization and I want to explore joining the Alliance!"
   - Route: Organization partnership

4. **Finance the Renaissance**
   - "I represent a Fund/Institution interested in Systemic ReGeneration and I want to explore funding the Alliance."
   - Route: Investor/funder inquiry

5. **Live**
   - "I want to co-create with one/some of the ReGen Land Projects"
   - Route: Community member/resident interest

6. **Role**
   - "Apply for a role in ReGen Civics directly"
   - Route: Job/volunteer application

7. **Something else?**
   - General inquiry/other interest
   - Route: Catch-all for other interests

## Path 1: APPLY For Next Season / Land Partner

### Questions:
1. Best link to explore your project? (URL input)
2. Tell us more about what inspired you to reach out! Feel free to also leave empty :) (Text area)
3. Where are you at with your project? (Checkboxes - select all that apply)
   - Team of 3+ committed core members.
   - Land under a structure with team having 100% authority.
   - Vision, Mission, Purpose, Values, etc clearly defined.
   - Governance, legal and economic system designed.
   - 3+ Families living on the land.
   - Producing 50%+ of own food.
   - Producing 50%+ own utilities.
   - Masterplan completed.
   - $1M+ USD invested.
