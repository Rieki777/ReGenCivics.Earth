# ReGen Civics Landing Page Redesign v2

## New Design Direction: Enchanted Forest Storybook

**Design Movement**: Illustrated fantasy meets cooperative game board

**Core Principles**:
- Full-bleed, vibrant imagery that commands attention
- Bold contrast between sections with distinct visual identities
- Playful yet grounded typography that feels hand-crafted
- No washed-out overlays - let the art shine

**Color Philosophy**: 
Deep forest greens (#1a472a) paired with warm golden amber (#d4a574) as primary accents. Rich earth tones ground the design while pops of coral (#e07a5f) and sky blue (#87ceeb) add energy. White text on dark sections, dark text on light sections - always high contrast.

**Typography System**:
Display: "Quicksand" (rounded, friendly, modern) - feels organic and approachable
Body: "Nunito" (soft, readable, warm) - comfortable for longer text
Accent: "Righteous" (bold, playful, game-like) - for CTAs and emphasis

**Layout Paradigm**: 
Full-width immersive sections that alternate between image-dominant and content-dominant. Hero takes the full viewport with the illustration as the star. Content sections use bold colored backgrounds (forest green, warm cream) rather than faded image overlays.

**Signature Elements**:
- Thick decorative borders around cards inspired by game boards
- Organic wave dividers between sections
- Bold button styling with visible borders and shadows
- Full-opacity hero image with text overlay using semi-transparent dark panel
