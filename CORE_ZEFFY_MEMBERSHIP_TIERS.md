# Church of the Regenerative Earth — Membership Tiers (Zeffy)

Draft copy for setting up recurring membership on Zeffy. Prices are a recommendation, not a fixed decision. Change any number and I will rebalance the tiers around it.

---

## What membership means here

Membership in the Church of the Regenerative Earth is a way of belonging to the land and to each other. Giving is worship. Every monthly gift pools into one purpose: buying and healing land, holding gatherings, keeping the elders and their wisdom close, recording the Hymns of the Regeneration, and running the platform where the community lives.

A few things members should understand before they join:

- **Your gift is a spiritual act, not a purchase of power.** Governance voice and ordination as a Magician are earned through participation and quests. They are never bought. Membership here is a patron circle. It funds the work and roots you in the community. It does not buy votes or rank.
- **All are welcome, at any amount.** No one is left behind the curtain and no one is left outside the gate. The tiers below are suggested resting places on a ladder. If a tier is out of reach, give what you can. The door is the same size for everyone.
- **You keep your other spiritual homes.** The Church is poly-religious. Joining does not ask you to leave any tradition you already hold.
- **Everything is held in the open.** Members can see where the money goes. Transparency is part of the worship.

The four circles below deepen like a landscape: a seed becomes roots, roots become a grove, a grove becomes a sanctuary. Each circle includes everything in the one before it.

## The four circles

### 1. Seed — $11 / month

*The all-welcome circle. Where belonging begins.*

**What you fund:** the ground itself. Land, gatherings, and the platform that holds the community.

**What you receive:**
- Your name held in the digital circle of members
- A monthly note from the Church with one of the Hymns of the Regeneration
- Access to the members' spaces on the ReGen Civics platform
- The open transparency report, so you can see where every gift goes

*Zeffy public description:* "Where belonging begins. Your monthly gift roots you in the community and funds the land, the gatherings, and the platform that holds us. Includes the members' circle, a monthly hymn, and full transparency into where your gift goes."

---

### 2. Root — $33 / month

*For those putting down roots. A deeper hold in the soil.*

**What you fund:** everything Seed funds, plus the making of new sacred music and the seasonal rhythm of the community.

**What you receive (everything in Seed, plus):**
- Early access to new Hymns of the Regeneration as they are recorded
- A seasonal letter from the ministers and elders
- Invitations to the online seasonal and new-moon gatherings

*Zeffy public description:* "For those putting down roots. Everything in Seed, plus early access to new hymns, a seasonal letter from the ministers and elders, and invitations to the community's online seasonal gatherings."

---

### 3. Grove — $77 / month

*For those who tend. A living canopy that shelters others.*

**What you fund:** everything Root funds, plus live wisdom circles and the elder teachings at the heart of the Church.

**What you receive (everything in Root, plus):**
- A live monthly wisdom circle with an elder or minister, drawing on the Anastasia teachings and the Church's creed
- Your name spoken in gratitude at the Church's land ceremonies
- A once-a-year blessed token from the land (a seed packet or small handmade item)

*Zeffy public description:* "For those who tend the whole. Everything in Root, plus a live monthly wisdom circle with an elder, your name spoken at land ceremonies, and a blessed token from the land each year."

---

### 4. Sanctuary — $111 / month

*For the deepest circle. Old growth that holds the forest.*

**What you fund:** everything Grove funds, plus the frontier of the mission: acquiring dedicated land and building physical sanctuaries.

**What you receive (everything in Grove, plus):**
- An invitation to in-person gatherings and ceremonies at the Church's land projects
- A personal welcome from a Founding Minister
- An invitation to the annual community retreat
- Recognition among the founding stewards of the land the Church acquires (recognition and gratitude, not ownership or control)

*Zeffy public description:* "The deepest circle, the old growth that holds the forest. Everything in Grove, plus invitations to in-person gatherings on the Church's land, a personal welcome from a Founding Minister, the annual retreat, and recognition among the founding stewards of the land we heal."

## One-time and open giving

Alongside the four monthly circles, keep two doors open for people who are not ready to commit monthly:

- **Give once:** a single gift of any amount, with a suggested set of $33 / $77 / $111 / $333.
- **Name your gift:** an open field so anyone can give exactly what feels right. This honors the "all are welcome, at any amount" promise and catches the people between tiers.

An annual option is worth offering too. A common gesture is ten months' price for a year of membership, so annual members get two months as a gift: Seed $110 / Root $330 / Grove $770 / Sanctuary $1,111 per year.

## Setting this up in Zeffy (notes)

- Zeffy's **Membership** form type lets you list these four as tiers with a name, price, frequency, and description. Paste the "Zeffy public description" lines above into each tier's description field.
- Set frequency to **monthly recurring** as the default. Add the annual amounts as a second frequency if Zeffy allows both on one form, or as a separate "Give annually" form.
- Turn on the **open / name-your-amount** field so no one is priced out.
- Zeffy charges nonprofits **zero platform fees**, so 100 percent of each gift reaches the Church. You can say this plainly on the form. It builds trust.
- Add a short line to the confirmation and receipt: the Church's legal name, EIN, and that gifts are tax-deductible as allowed by law under its 508(c)(1)(a) status. (Use the exact receipt language once legal has reviewed it, per the CORE handoff.)

## Two flags worth a human eye

- **Tax deductibility and thank-you gifts.** For a gift to stay fully tax-deductible, the benefits a donor receives in return must be intangible or of insubstantial value under IRS thresholds. The intangible benefits above (hymns, letters, wisdom circles, invitations, recognition, transparency) are safe. The one physical item, the Grove "blessed token," is the only benefit that can reduce the deductible amount. Keep it genuinely nominal, or disclose its fair market value on the receipt. A lawyer or accountant should confirm this before real gifts flow. This sits inside the legal review already on your list.
- **Do not sell governance or ordination.** The tiers deliberately avoid granting RGVoice, Citizen status, or Magician ordination, because those are earned and would be both doctrinally and legally fraught to sell. If you ever want a tier to unlock participation faster, route it through quests and community recognition, not the price.
