## The Comparison: Human vs Nature Food Production

**US Food Production (Human-designed):**
- 96 Billion Lbs of Wheat
- 67 Billion Lbs Corn
- 218 Billion Lbs Milk
- Total: Nearly 1 TRILLION lbs

**Nature's Food Production (American Chestnut):**
- A single mature American Chestnut Tree produces ~3000 lbs of chestnut fruit/nut per acre
- There were once 3-4 Billion of these trees in Eastern US forests
- Result: 9-12 TRILLION lbs FREE FOOD FALLING FROM THE SKY EACH YEAR!

(Note: Comments corrected this to 50-100 lbs per tree, so ~200-400 billion lbs - still massive)

## What Happened?

**Salmon:** We dammed the rivers and stopped the Salmon from bringing ocean nutrients (like nitrogen) deep into forests. "Salmon so numerous you could cross the river on their backs" - Native American saying

**Beavers:** We hunted them for fur (colonizers loved fur hats). They managed water cycles, making slower/flatter water terrain for salmon. Est. 300m+ then, <13m now

**Bears:** We hunted them for skin, flesh, sport. They carried salmon deep into forests, nurturing chestnut trees. Bears are food foresters! Est. 2m+ then, <300k now

**Today:** <10k Great American Chestnut trees alive today (near extinction)
