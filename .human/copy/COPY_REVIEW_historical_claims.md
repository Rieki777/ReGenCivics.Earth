### How it works (3-step summary)

1. **Answer a few questions.** A 10-minute guided conversation about your work, what you've built, and what you're bringing.
2. **Get your tier.** Based on your answers, we suggest a tier and a $ReGen token allocation.
3. **Present at a Proposal Party.** Share your story with the community. Collectively we'll confirm your tier and your allocation goes live! Along with token recognition we'll share your introduction and what you're bringing here so we can all get to know the collective value we have in our new economies.

It marks the people and organizations who showed up at the start: before the economy was live, before the network was built, in the particular moment we all stepped into together.

Future contributors are welcome and valued. They're not Founders.

The founding round closes at the September equinox 2026.

## Claim Flow: Screen-by-Screen Copy

### Screen 1: Welcome

We're one network, built by hundreds of people and organizations who've been doing regenerative work for years. Some for decades.

This is the founding round. Every contributor recognized here becomes a Founder of ReGen Civics. That's a permanent designation that future rounds won't carry.

This is how we recognize what you've done and what you're bringing.

It takes about 10 minutes. At the end, you'll know where you fit and how your work connects to the movement.

**Button:** Let's go

---

### Screen 2: Who are you?

Are you claiming as an individual or on behalf of an organization?

**Buttons:** Individual / Organization

---

### Screen 3: What's your name?

**Individual version:** Your name and handle (or the name you go by in the movement).

**Organization version:** Your organization's name and a one-line description of what you do.

---

### Screen 4: What forms of capital are you bringing?

The regenerative movement runs on more than money. We recognize 8 forms of capital. Pick every form that describes what you're contributing. Most people bring several.

**Checkbox labels and descriptions:**

- **Intellectual:** Research, writing, curricula, frameworks, methodologies, publications, open-source code
- **Experiential:** Years of practice, skills earned through doing, apprenticeships, hands-on knowledge you carry in your body and your work
- **Social:** Relationships, networks, trust, community connections, the ability to bring people together and hold space
- **Material:** Tools, technology, platforms, physical infrastructure, land access, equipment, built things others can use
- **Financial:** Fund design, grant programs, economic models, currency experiments, investment infrastructure
- **Living (Ecological):** Land stewardship, regenerative agriculture, food production, conservation, seed saving, ecosystem restoration
- **Cultural:** Art, storytelling, music, media, ceremony, language, traditions that carry meaning through the movement
- **Spiritual:** Healing practices, wellness, contemplative traditions, sacred relationship with land and community

**Follow-up prompt (appears for each selected form):** Give a one-line example.

**Follow-up placeholder examples:** "Built a soil carbon measurement tool" / "15 years facilitating community circles"

---

### Screen 5: How long have you been at it?

**Individual version:** How long have you been actively contributing to regenerative work?

**Organization version:** How long has your organization been operating?

**Options:** Under 1 year / 1-3 years / 3-5 years / 5-10 years / 10+ years

---

### Screen 6: Who have you reached?

How many people has your work directly served, taught, connected, or supported?

**Individual options:** Under 50 / 50-200 / 200-1,000 / 1,000+ / Hard to count, it's generational

**Organization options:** Under 500 / 500-5,000 / 5,000+

---

### Screen 7: What have you built?

What tangible things have you created that others can use? Pick all that apply.

**Checkbox labels:**

- A tool, software, or platform
- A curriculum, course, or training program
- A methodology or framework
- Templates, guides, or documentation
- A physical space, farm, or land project
- A network, community, or organizing structure
- Publications, books, or research
- Art, media, or cultural work
- Financial infrastructure (fund, grant program, currency)
- I've contributed time and energy but haven't built a named thing (that's real too)

**Follow-up prompt:** Give it a name and a one-sentence description.

**Helper text:** If you built five things, name the top two or three. You can skip the rest.

---

### Screen 8: Tell us more

In your own words, describe what you've done and what you're bringing to this network. A few sentences is fine. A few paragraphs is fine too.

---

### Screen 9: Evidence

If you have links to share (website, project page, publication, photos), drop them here. Totally optional.

**Helper text:** Many contributors do their best work offline or in spaces that don't have URLs. That's fine.

---

### Screen 10: What's alive for you now?

One sentence: what are you bringing to this network going forward?

---

### Screen 11: Your suggested tier (standard)

Based on what you've shared, we'd suggest you're at the **[Tier Name]** level.

[Tier description from the rubric]

This means your historical contribution claim would be for **[X] $ReGen tokens** (valued at $[Y] USD at $0.01/token), locked until the economy goes live, then vesting over 18 months.

As a first-round contributor, you'll also receive the permanent **Founder** designation. That's separate from your tier and carries regardless of which tier you're in.

Does this feel right?

**Buttons:** Yes, that's right / I think I should be higher (explain why) / I think I should be lower

**Higher override prompt:** Tell us why you think your contributions place you at a higher tier.

---

### Screen 11: Above-tier variant

Based on what you've shared, your contributions are at a scale that goes beyond our standard tiers. That's a good thing. It means what you've built is significant enough that it deserves a different kind of relationship with the network.

Two paths forward:

**Claim as an organization.** If your work isn't already organized as an entity, this might be the moment. Contributions at this scale usually benefit from collective stewardship: a team, a board, a structure that can manage and grow what you've built. Our organization tiers go up to $1M.

**Token swap and join the Fund.** For contributions valued above the tier system, we invite you to explore a direct partnership with the ReGen Civics Fund. This means a token swap (your existing assets, IP, or infrastructure for $ReGen at a negotiated rate) and a seat at the fund level. This is how the network's biggest contributors become its economic partners.

**Buttons:** Claim as an organization / Learn about the Fund pathway / Continue with individual tiers anyway

---

### Screen 12: Where you fit in the network

Here's how your contributions connect to the network:

**Material or Intellectual capital with tangible outputs:**
Your [tool name] is being added to our Tools Library so the whole movement can find and use it. You'll be able to update its description, add usage notes, and see how the community is using it.

**Living (Ecological) capital, or food/land/agriculture outputs:**
We're partnering with Local Scale for bioregional food-backed economies. You can list your offerings there and accept a percentage in $ReGen tokens. That counts as ongoing contribution to the network.

**Link:** Learn about Local Scale ->

**Social or Financial capital:**
Your experience is exactly what our governance system needs. Once you're recognized, you'll have governance weight to participate in decisions that shape this movement.

**Experiential capital with teaching/mentoring examples:**
We run seasonal learning programs where experienced practitioners mentor newer players. Your expertise has a home here.

**For everyone:**
Your claim will be reviewed by our team and you'll be invited to the next Proposal Party, where you'll introduce yourself and your work to the community.

---

### Screen 13: Help us evolve this game

You just went through our contribution recognition process. You saw how it works from the inside. Here's the thing: this process is itself part of the Game, and the Game is co-created by everyone who plays it.

Did anything feel off? Do you see a way to make this more fair, more clear, more useful? Maybe a question we should be asking that we didn't. Maybe a tier distinction that doesn't make sense. Maybe a whole category of contribution we're missing.

If something came to mind, share it here. Your idea will become a thread in our forum where the community can build on it together.

**Textarea placeholder:** What would you change or add?

**Buttons:** Submit idea to the forum / Skip for now

---

### Screen 14: The Proposal Party invitation

Your claim is saved. You're in the founding round.

Here's what happens next:

1. Our team will review your claim within the next 7 days.
2. You'll get a notification when your review is complete, along with any feedback.
3. Proposal Parties start at the **June Solstice**. We'll notify you when the first ones are scheduled. Add our calendar so you don't miss it.

The first claim window is open until the September equinox. Proposal Parties are live events, equal parts introduction party and governance. You'll have 3 minutes to share who you are, what you've built, and what you're bringing. The community confirms your tier, your $ReGen allocation becomes official, and your Founder badge goes live on your profile.

Between now and then, your claim is visible to our review team but not yet on the public Contributors page. After the Proposal Party, everything goes live.

**Buttons:** Save my claim / Add calendar / Edit something first

## Tier Names and Descriptions

### Individual Tiers

**Seed ($750 USD / 75,000 $ReGen)**
You've started your regenerative journey. You've taken courses, volunteered, participated in community projects, or begun integrating regenerative practices into your life and work.

**Sprout ($3,500 USD / 350,000 $ReGen)**
You're actively contributing. You've built something, taught others, or organized your community around regenerative practice.

**Sapling ($15,000 USD / 1,500,000 $ReGen)**
You're a recognized practitioner or contributor. People in your bioregion or domain know your work.

**Grove ($50,000 USD / 5,000,000 $ReGen)**
You're a leader in your domain. Your work has influenced how others practice and organize.

**Old Growth ($150,000 USD / 15,000,000 $ReGen)**
You're a root system of the movement. Your contributions span years or decades and have shaped how regenerative work is understood and practiced.

### Organization Tiers

**Roots ($20,000 USD / 2,000,000 $ReGen)**
Organizations that have built something real but are early in their journey or operating at a small scale.

**Canopy ($200,000 USD / 20,000,000 $ReGen)**
Organizations with established programs, growing communities, and proven track records.

**Mycelium ($1,000,000 USD / 100,000,000 $ReGen)**
Organizations that are infrastructure for the movement. Networks of networks. The ones that, if they disappeared, hundreds of other projects would feel the gap.
