## Thread: Why ReGen Civics Has Two Sides — and Why That's the Whole Point

Something I want to name directly, because it shapes everything else about how ReGen Civics is designed.

We are not asking you to choose between the world as it is and the world we're building. We're building a bridge between them. And a bridge, by nature, needs to be anchored on both sides.

---

**The chasm**

There are two Games being played right now.

The first is the **Dominant Game**: the economic, legal, and political system most of us were born into. Extractive, growth-at-all-costs, organized around capital accumulation and centralized power. It's the system that built the institutions, the financial instruments, the legal structures that govern most of the world's resources right now. It is showing its cracks. But it is still, for now, where most capital lives.

The second is a **growing diversity of new Games**: regenerative economies, decentralized governance, community-owned land, ways of organizing human life that are oriented toward healing rather than extraction. These are not one unified system — they are many experiments, many communities, many approaches. They are growing. They are often underfunded. And they are the future.

Between these two Games is a chasm. People on one side can barely see the other. Crossing it takes courage, knowledge, and usually a guide.

ReGen Civics is trying to be the bridge.

---

**Two distinct spaces**

To build that bridge, we've deliberately anchored into both sides.

**The ReGen Civics Fund** is our anchor in the Dominant Game. It uses one of the most powerful coordination structures the old system has produced: a venture capital fund. This isn't a compromise of our values — it's a strategic choice. To move serious capital into regenerative land projects, we need to speak the language of institutions and investors who live in the old Game. So we built a structure they can understand, trust, and engage with.

The Fund is governed by **RCVoice tokens**. Contributions to the Fund are tracked and rewarded in **$RCivics**. RCVoice determines how the Fund's profits and distributions are allocated. It operates by rules the old Game recognizes — rigorously, transparently, with on-chain accountability.

**The ReGen Civics Game** is our anchor in the new Games. It's where we experiment, play, and build together. It's governed by **RGVoice tokens**, earned through genuine participation — not purchased, not inherited. Contributions to the Game are recognized in **$ReGen**, a token whose utility we are actively co-creating.

What $ReGen becomes is an open question we're holding together. It could be an in-game currency. A tracker for how donations and investments flow into the Game. A rewards token where holding more earns you more rights and powers. A currency for day-to-day interactions in the ecosystem. All of the above, in different seasons. The key thing is that $ReGen is *not* tied to the logic of the old Game — that's what the Fund and $RCivics are for. $ReGen gets to be something new.

---

**How they work together**

These two spaces are separate by design. The Fund needs to operate within old Game rules to be effective there. The Game needs freedom from those rules to become something genuinely new.

But they're not in opposition. They're two anchors for the same bridge.

When the Fund raises capital and deploys it into regenerative land projects, it creates real-world proof that this model works — in language the mainstream understands. When the Game grows and deepens, it creates the culture, the community, the governance wisdom, and the lived experience of what regenerative civilization actually feels like. Each side makes the other more possible.

Part of what the Game is building is the bridge itself: better maps, better guides, better on-ramps for the growing number of people ready to cross. Because the goal isn't to keep people in either Game forever. The goal is to help as many people as possible walk from the old world into the new one — safely, joyfully, and, if we do this right, with a bit of awe and wonder.

---

**What this means for you**

If you're here as an investor, a funder, or an institution: the Fund is the right entry point. It speaks your language. It has the structure and accountability you need. And it connects your capital to land projects that are doing real work.

If you're here as a community member, a land steward, a regenerative practitioner, a curious person: the Game is yours. Earn RGVoice by showing up. Shape what $ReGen becomes. Build the new Game with us.

If you're here as both — working inside existing institutions while dreaming of something different — you're exactly who this bridge is for.
