### Tool 1: Contribution Scores (line 223-240)
**Title:** Contribution Scores
**Paragraph 1:** We track a growing range of contributions: quests completed, players onboarded, land projects supported, transactions facilitated, proposals passed, forum engagement, events attended, food produced and distributed and a growing diversity of regenerative actions.
**Paragraph 2:** Each action earns points. Points are ranked against all other players on a percentile scale from 0 to 99. Your score determines your share of the seasonal Harvest and your governance voice. This is our way of creating a sort of UBI (universal basic income) that's distributed for playing the regenerative Game (Aka doing the regenerative work we need to do to thrive as a species).
**Paragraph 3:** The percentile system is the key. A billionaire and a community gardener are scored on the same 0 to 99 scale. Hoarding has diminishing returns. Contributing does not. Over time, wealth distributes outward. Scores decay, so active contributors earn more than people resting on history.
**Paragraph 4:** What counts as a "contribution" is up to us. The community decides through governance what to track, what to reward, and how to weight it (how much it matters to us).

### Tool 2: Gratitude (line 257-271)
**Title:** Gratitude
**Subtitle:** A hug benefits both the giver and receiver. What if our currency did the same?
**Paragraph 1:** Some of the most valuable things people do are invisible to any tracking system. Caring for elders. Making art. Holding space after loss. Teaching for free because it needed sharing. Growing food for neighbors.
**Paragraph 2:** Each cycle (roughly 29.5 days, tied to lunar rhythms from new moon to new moon), you receive a budget of gratitude tokens for you to give out. Send them to people for anything you're grateful for. Holding gratitude does nothing. It activates when sent.
**Paragraph 3:** At cycle's end, a pot of $ReGen tokens are distributed proportionally to the people who received the most gratitude. Acknowledgment becomes economic value. The more people participating, the more accurately value distributes. This is our start in Swarm Intelligence applied to meeting each other's needs.

### Tool 3: Proposals (line 288-311)
**Title:** Proposals
**Paragraph 1:** Fund a land project. Adjust a harvest ratio. Create a new quest category. Change a contribution metric. Allocate resources to start a Bioregional Financing Facility (BFF)...  While anyone can make a proposal, we invite you to obtain the status of "Steward" or "Sage" before you do, and to be thoughtful and complete in what you bring, as every formal proposal is a request of the community's shared attention.

**Paragraph 2:** Proposals go to a vote. All players vote - or delegate their Voice to someone they chose- with our earned RGVoice (governance tokens earned through contribution). If a proposal passes quorum and unity thresholds, it executes! Voice is proportional to contribution, not wealth. The more you play, the more Voice you have!


**Callout:** Together, the three tools provide a foundation for us to coordinate with. Proposals for collective decisions. Contribution Scores for quantitative coordination. Gratitude for qualitative coordination. What we build on top of this foundation (better and better tools and Games) is up to all of us to decide. This is the "Infinite Game".
