# Token Rebalancing Proposal — All Quests
*Drafted 2026-03-14 for Rye's review*

**Principle:** 111 $ReGen is the baseline for a standard quest (2-4 weeks of real practice, meaningful output). We move in either direction based on genuine complexity, time, and what the quest asks of a person. 1 RGVoice is always 1 per quest regardless.

**Scale used:**
- 33 = gentle (brief, light, short)
- 77 = easy (focused hours, simple output)
- 99 = accessible (one week of practice)
- 111 = standard baseline
- 144 = deep (one full month, real documentation)
- 177 = significant (multi-month or lasting creation)
- 222 = advanced (sustained effort, high-quality output, skill required)
- 333 = transformative (multi-month, real-world impact)
- 444+ = hero quest (life-stage commitment)

## Notes for Rye

- **Quest 13 (Fasting) as repeatable at 77:** The idea is that fasting is a practice that gets richer over time, and keeping the per-fast reward modest encourages people to do it regularly rather than once for the tokens. Total earnings can compound significantly for dedicated fasters.
- **Present Parenting at 444:** This is a genuine Hero Quest -- years of prioritized presence. It deserves to be the highest-reward quest on the site. Consider whether there's also a special badge, forum role, or title for people who complete it.
- **ReGen Financial Systems at 333:** Launching an actual community currency is arguably harder than any other quest in the set. The EPIC quest comparison (Block Food Forest = Easy Mode EPIC) gives some calibration. 333 feels right.
- **Medicine Journey at 222:** This is the quest that asks the most of a person emotionally and spiritually. It deserves significantly higher recognition than baseline.
- These are proposals, not finalized. Change any amount that doesn't feel right. The logic is here so the reasoning is clear.
