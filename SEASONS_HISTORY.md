# ReGen Civics: Seasons History

A living index of every season's roles, characters, compensation, and lessons. Full details for each season live in `seasons/season-N-name.md`. We learn from this history to evolve the roles, the art, the compensation, and the game itself.

---

## Compensation Model

**Token value:** $ReGen is valued at **$0.01 each** (benchmark established Season 1).

**7 Bands** (base per season, before Harvest bonuses):

| Band | $ReGen/Season | USD Equivalent | Effective $/hr Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 900,000 | $9,000 | ~$46/hr at 15 hrs/wk |
| 6 | 800,000 | $8,000 | ~$51/hr at 12 hrs/wk |
| 5 | 700,000 | $7,000 | ~$54/hr at 10 hrs/wk |
| 4 | 600,000 | $6,000 | ~$46/hr at 10 hrs/wk |
| 3 | 500,000 | $5,000 | ~$38/hr at 10 hrs/wk |
| 2 | 400,000 | $4,000 | ~$38/hr at 8 hrs/wk |
| 1 | 300,000 | $3,000 | ~$38/hr at 6 hrs/wk |

**Seed and Harvest bonus model:** Each role has a Seed (deliverable commitment) and a Harvest (ecosystem impact). Plant seeds and the harvest comes in = +30% bonus. One without the other = +15%. See individual season files for per-role details.

**3:1 ratio:** The highest band is 3x the lowest. Steps of 100,000 $ReGen between each band.

## Season Index

### Season 1: The First Build
- **Dates:** Winter 2025-2026 (Dec 2025 to Spring Equinox 2026)
- **Theme:** Building and Preparing
- **Roles:** 14 (4 partially filled, 10 open)
- **Art style:** Solarpunk / elven / regenerative future
- **Key changes:** First season. Established the roles, compensation bands, Seed/Harvest model. Assembly Steward (role 14) added mid-season when the Assembly shipped 2026-07-03.
- **Total base budget:** 7,800,000 $ReGen ($78,000) per season across all 14 roles
