# Quest Page Content Notes

## Overview
- Quests are fun, magical, playful, productive, and regenerative ways to participate in creating beautiful civilizations
- Curated by active members of the ReGen Civics Alliance
- Earn tokens ($Regen utility tokens and RVoice governance tokens) for completing quests

## Quest Categories

### Open Quests
- Simple quests co-created by membership

### Rites of Passage Quests
- Designed for GREATEST REGENERATIVE IMPACT with LOWEST EFFORT
- Can be done in any order, multiple times (up to 3x rewards)
- Focus on having fun while deepening relationships

## Why Quests Help Us:
1. Heal Ourselves (body, family, community, bioregion, Earth)
2. Grow and learn together (sharing journey with community)
3. Distribute ownership and governance rights (more quests = more tokens = more voice)
4. Co-create an evolutionary Game
5. Regenerate relationships with each other and more-than-human world
