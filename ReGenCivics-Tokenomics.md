# ReGen Civics Tokenomics

## Token Economics for the Regenerative Renaissance

> **Tokenomics = Token Economics.** Tokens are the things we create through our governance process to account for things. And economics? The word comes from the Ancient Greek *oikonomia* (οἰκονομία) - a compound of *oikos* (οἶκος), meaning "household," and *nomos* (νόμος), meaning "law" or "custom." It originally meant "household management." In our case, our token economic system sees Earth as our home - and we're designing an economic system for how we can go about caring for our home together.

## Two Tokens, Two Powers

The ReGen Civics Fund and Alliance runs on two distinct but complementary tokens (separate from the Game tokens). Understanding the difference between them is the foundation of everything else.

| Token | What It Does | How You Get It | Is It Tradable? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **RCVoice** | Governs fund decisions | Earned through active contribution | No — non-transferable |
| **$RCivics** | Claims on rewards and returns | Investment, equity swap, or contribution | Yes — tradable utility token |

**RCVoice** is your say in how the fund is governed. It cannot be bought or sold. To learn about how it's distributed visit the /governance page.

**$RCivics** is your economic stake in the ecosystem. It represents a proportional claim on portfolio returns, and the growing value of the regenerative land and organisations that make up the ReGen Civics portfolio. Think of it as an index token for the Regenerative Renaissance - a single token that holds a stake in every project and organisation in the alliance.

## What Is $RCivics?

$RCivics is a progressively maturing token with four stacking functions that unlock over time as the ecosystem grows. It starts as an acknowledgement of contributions and matures into a full ecosystem coordination and liquidity token.

### Phase 1: Ecosystem Acknowledgement Token

The first function of $RCivics is to acknowledge and reciprocate contributions to the Regenerative Renaissance. Many people and organisations have been building this movement for years without having their contributions formally accounted for. $RCivics is designed to change that.

A diversity of forms of capital can be contributed to the alliance and acknowledged with $RCivics tokens:

- **Knowledge Capital** — templates, research, frameworks, documentation

- **Material Capital** — tools, software, infrastructure, physical resources

- **Natural Capital** — land offered as a future ReGen Civics project site

- **Experience Capital** — facilitation, alliance support, mentorship

- **Financial Capital** — direct investment in cash or crypto equivalents

$RCivics starts with a token balance of zero. New tokens are minted only when new contributions are formally acknowledged through the governance process. In this way, the number of $RCivics in circulation is directly tied to the actual contributions to the movement - not to speculation or arbitrary issuance.

**The CHF Peg:** In the early years, $RCivics is valued at 1 CHF (Swiss Franc) per token. We chose the Swiss Franc because, as of early 2026, it is widely considered the world's most stable currency -underpinned by Switzerland's strong economy, low unemployment, high GDP per capita, political neutrality, and sound monetary policy. This peg gives us a stable, globally neutral reference point before we develop our own monetary policy as the ecosystem matures.

### Phase 2: Ecosystem Index / Investment Token

As the portfolio of land projects and alliance organisations grows, $RCivics matures into an index token for the Regenerative Renaissance.

When financial contributions are made to ReGen Civics, those funds are not held in a treasury - they are directed towards regenerative land projects in exchange for those projects' tokens. In this way, every $RCivics token is backed by a growing portfolio of land-backed and regenerative assets.

**Token Swaps:** The primary mechanism for building this portfolio is the token swap - when two organisations exchange equity or value-backed tokens of equal value. For example, if a land project has a valuation of €15,000,000, they could swap €1,000,000 in their project tokens for €1,000,000 in $RCivics. Both organisations now hold a stake in each other. Both benefit when the other grows.

This is how $RCivics becomes an index: as more projects and alliance organisations join and swap tokens, $RCivics comes to represent a proportional stake in every organisation in the ecosystem. Investing in $RCivics is investing in the entire regenerative movement.

**Backed by land - growing with regeneration:** This creates the backing behind the token. And because many project tokens are directly connected to the value of the land they steward, as we regenerate land and communities, the backing behind $RCivics increases in value. A food forest producing abundant yields is worth more than degraded farmland. Regeneration is the return.

**ReGen Civics does not hoard surplus.** As an alliance organisation, all surplus generated from fees, yields, or contributions is reinvested into alliance organisations and projects. ReGen Civics is not a business - it is coordination infrastructure for the regenerative movement.

### Phase 3: Ecosystem Liquidity Token

As token swaps are established across the portfolio, $RCivics matures into the interchange token for the entire alliance ecosystem.

When two projects conduct a token swap, both parties set up **liquidity pools** - always-available exchanges where community members can trade between project tokens and $RCivics. Both parties commit to locking their liquidity, ensuring stability and preventing either party from dumping the other's tokens.

This creates a powerful network effect: because every project in the alliance has a liquidity pool with $RCivics, any token in the ecosystem can be converted to any other token by routing through $RCivics. You can spend your tokens from one project at another project, automatically and behind the scenes, without either project needing to set up a direct exchange with each other.

[Callout] So, as we travel the world and visit other projects we can seamlessly spend and use the tokens we earn in one project at any other within the alliance.

All trading yields and fees generated from $RCivics liquidity pools are reinvested into regeneration - used to buy more land-backed project tokens and grow the portfolio. As the ecosystem matures and more exchanges take place, this creates a self-sustaining revenue stream that funds the movement.

### Phase 4: Ecosystem Access Token

In the future, organisations using the ReGen Civics ecosystem of tools will be invited to stake (lock up) $RCivics tokens to access the full suite of alliance services. This creates a steady, organic demand for $RCivics as more projects join the ecosystem - without the extractive fee structures that characterise most tech platforms.

Alliance organisations and seasonal projects are encouraged to hold their $RCivics tokens even after the liquidity lock expires (TBD), reserving them for staking to retain lifetime access to the ecosystem's tools and benefits.

## How Returns Are Distributed

Profit Distributions / Dividends are distributed proportionally to **$RCivics token holders** - not to voice holders. Your economic stake in the ecosystem is determined by how many $RCivics tokens you hold, not by how much governance voice you have.

Separately Fund management fees are distributed as follows:

- **50% to the Council of Domain Experts** - reflecting their operational leadership, oversight, and the majority of the ongoing work of stewarding the fund. Part of the Council's allocation funds worker proposals to ReGen Civics contributors.

- **50% proportionally to all RCVoice holders** - distributed across all four governance groups relative to their voice holdings.

This structure ensures that those doing the most work are compensated appropriately, while all contributors to the governance process share in the management of the fund.

## The Governance Token: RCVoice

$RCivics is **not** a governance token. Governance in ReGen Civics is handled by RCVoice - a non-tradable token that can only be earned through active participation and contribution to the alliance.

Voice is distributed across four groups:

- **Council of Domain Experts:** 40%

- **Land Projects:** 20%

- **Alliance Organisations:** 20%

- **Investors:** 20%

All proposals to create new $RCivics tokens require a **90% unity threshold** and **80% quorum** to pass. This strict threshold is designed to foster complete, well-considered proposals that carry genuine support across the diversity of the alliance.

## How Resources Flow Through the Ecosystem

One of the most powerful features of the ReGen Civics model is the ability to intelligently direct resources across the movement in response to what the ecosystem needs.

Just as a body sends more resources to the legs when running and more to the stomach when digesting, ReGen Civics can dynamically redirect resources to the organisations and projects that need them most in each season. During a Summer Season focused on festival building, more resources flow to festival support organisations. During a Spring Season focused on project incubation, more resources flow to land projects.

This is governed through the seasonal voting process - a 6-week cycle of community discussion, festival dialogue and alignment / voting, and implementation - ensuring that resource allocation decisions are made by the people closest to the work.

**Alliance Organisations do the work Land Projects need.** A core design principle of the network is that land projects should use their funds to pay alliance organisations for services wherever possible - keeping resources circulating within the ecosystem rather than flowing out to external providers. Legal support, ecological design, technology infrastructure, financial modelling, organisational development, etc... all of these services exist within the alliance, and every payment within the network strengthens the whole.

## A Multi-Generational Journey

We don't focus on market cycles, bull runs, or token valuations. We measure our success by our shared progress towards co-creating regenerative cultures and civilizations.

Much like planting mighty nut-bearing trees is a gift our descendants most benefit from, our efforts to create the foundations for new civilizations will be most enjoyed by those who come after us. This is a multi-generational journey - and the most important thing we get to do along the way is raise our children in cultures where we are actively redesigning what civilization looks like.

The token economic system we are building is not designed to extract value from the movement. It is designed to keep value circulating within it - rewarding those who contribute, backing the token with the land and communities we regenerate, and creating a financial system that is genuinely by the people and for the planet.
