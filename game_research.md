# ReGen Civics Game Research

## The Infinite Game Overview
- Welcome to the "ReGen Magicians Game"
- 4 ways to play: Quest, Learn, Ally, Apply

## R-Voice: Co-Create the Game / Governance
- Non-tradable Voice Token representing "weight your voice carries"
- More tokens = more powerful votes on proposals
- Different take on democracy where not everyone's voice is equal
- Players earn R-Voice by:
  - Completing Quests
  - Claiming Contribution Requests (sharing contributions, assigning value, making proposals)
- Quests designed to help people become more connected with life through direct experiences
- Game governed by those who've gained the most wisdom

## Quest Tokens ($Regen / $RCivics): Co-Own the Game / Economics
- Tradable Utility Tokens
- Earned by completing quests
- Example rewards:
  - Food Foresting Quest: +33 $Regen, +1 RVoice
  - Potion Brewing Quest: +111 $Regen, +1 RVoice
  - Saving Seeds Quest: +22 $Regen, +1 RVoice
  - Healing Wholes Quest: +111 $Regen, +1 RVoice
  - Fire Quest (intro): +111 $Regen, +1 RVoice

## Quest Structure
- Seasonal organization: Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter, Routine
- Each quest has:
  - Focus Relationships (e.g., Bioregion, Food, Health)
  - Deliverable (usually video/article)
  - Rewards (tokens)
  - Expected community gain

## Why Focus on Regeneration?
- ReGen Civics is a decentralised organisation
- Coordinates global network of regenerative land projects
- Thesis: To co-create regenerative civilization, we need to LIVE regeneratively today
- Focus on growing shared potential through healthier, more capable humans
- Creating alternatives to financial, economic, and governance systems of dominant empires

## Game Philosophy
- Fun, magical, playful, productive, and regenerative participation
- Ever-growing and ever-changing list of Quests curated by active members
- Designed for GREATEST REGENERATIVE IMPACT with LOWEST EFFORT
- Quests help us:
  - Heal ourselves (bodies, families, communities, bioregions, Earth)
  - Grow and learn together
  - Distribute ownership and governance rights
  - Co-create a beautiful, challenging, evolutionary game
  - Regenerate relationships with each other and more-than-human world
