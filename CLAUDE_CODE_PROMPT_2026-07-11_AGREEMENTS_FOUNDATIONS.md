Always attributed: "from The Four Agreements and The Fifth Agreement by don Miguel Ruiz and don Jose Ruiz." Summaries are ours, in our words:

1. **Be impeccable with your word.** Words create worlds. Speak with integrity, say only what you mean, and use your word in the direction of truth and love
2. **Don't take anything personally.** What others say and do is a projection of their own reality. Immunity to opinion ends a world of needless suffering
3. **Don't make assumptions.** Ask. Communicate clearly. Most drama is a story we invented and then believed
4. **Always do your best.** Your best changes moment to moment. Do it anyway, and self-judgment loses its grip
5. **Be skeptical, but learn to listen.** Don't believe yourself or anybody else blindly; hear the intent beneath the words and let truth reveal itself
