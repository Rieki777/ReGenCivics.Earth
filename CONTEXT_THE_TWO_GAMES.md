# Context: The Two Games — Fund and Game Distinction

*A reference note for the ReGen Civics team and for Claude when working on any content, copy, or code related to this project.*

---

## The Core Frame

ReGen Civics operates on both sides of a chasm.

On one side: **the Dominant Game** — the existing economic, political, and social systems built around extraction, growth-at-all-costs, and centralized power. Most institutions, investors, legal structures, and financial instruments live here.

On the other side: **a growing diversity of new Games** — regenerative economic systems, decentralized governance, community-owned land, and ways of organizing human life that are oriented toward healing rather than extraction.

ReGen Civics doesn't choose one side. It intentionally anchors into both — and builds the bridge between them.

---

## The Two Distinct Spaces

### 1. The Fund (Old Game Side)

The **ReGen Civics Fund** is anchored in the Dominant Game by design.

It uses one of the most powerful coordination structures the old Game has produced: **a venture capital fund**. This structure is legible to institutional investors, existing legal systems, and mainstream capital markets. That legibility is a feature, not a compromise. It lets us move real capital into regenerative projects using tools that the old Game understands and trusts.

**Governance:** RCVoice tokens
**Economic token:** $RCivics
**Purpose:** Determines how to allocate fund profits and distributions

RCVoice is how contributors earn governance weight over the Fund's financial decisions. $RCivics tracks and rewards contributions to the Fund. Together they create a transparent, accountable structure for stewarding capital — one that plays by enough of the old Game's rules to be effective within it, while bending those rules toward regenerative ends.

### 2. The Game (New Games Side)

The **ReGen Civics Game** is anchored in the new Games — plural, diverse, emergent, and co-created.

The Game is not tied to the old Game's economic logic. It is where we experiment, play, and build the systems that don't exist yet. It's rooted in community participation, regenerative values, and the belief that the people closest to the land and the work should hold real governance power.

**Governance:** RGVoice tokens
**Economic token:** $ReGen
**Purpose:** Governs how the Game evolves and co-creates the utility of $ReGen

RGVoice is earned through participation: completing quests, contributing to the community, engaging with the ecosystem. It gives weight to those who show up. $ReGen is the currency of the new Games — and its utility is something we are actively co-creating together.

**Current ideas for $ReGen utility** (open to community input):
- In-game currency for day-to-day interactions within the ecosystem
- Tracks how donations and investments flow into the Game
- Rewards token: the more you hold, the more rights and powers you earn
- Future governance mechanisms we haven't designed yet

The key point: $ReGen is not tied to the old Game. That is exactly the point. The Fund and $RCivics handle the old Game interface. $ReGen is free to become something new.

---

## How They Work Together

Think of it as two anchors on either side of a chasm.

The Fund is planted firmly in the old Game — it can speak the language of capital, attract institutional investment, and use legal structures that the mainstream understands. The Game is planted in the emerging new Games — it can experiment freely, reward contribution over capital, and co-create systems that don't have to make sense to Wall Street.

Together they form a bridge.

The bridge is what ReGen Civics is actually building. Part of the Game's purpose is to make that bridge bigger, stronger, and easier to cross — so that the growing number of people ready for these new realities can walk across safely, joyfully, and (ideally) with a bit of awe and wonder.

## Writing and Communication Notes

When writing about **The Fund:**
- Emphasize: capital, land-backed security, accountability, transparency, bioregional diversification, real financial returns alongside ecological impact
- RCVoice = governance over financial decisions
- $RCivics = how contributions to the Fund are tracked and rewarded
- Tone: rigorous, trustworthy, ambitious, grounded

When writing about **The Game:**
- Emphasize: community, participation, quests, co-creation, seasonal rhythms, emergence, the infinite game
- RGVoice = governance over how the Game evolves
- $ReGen = the currency of the future we're building together, utility to be co-created
- Tone: warm, playful, inviting, generative

When writing about **both together:**
- The bridge metaphor is powerful and true — use it
- "Two sides of a chasm" conveys the intentionality
- "Mastering both Games simultaneously" captures the ambition
- The goal: people can walk across the bridge safely, joyfully, with awe and wonder
