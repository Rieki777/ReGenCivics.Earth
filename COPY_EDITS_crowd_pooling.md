## Why Crowd Pooling? (collapsible panel)

**Section heading:** Why Crowd Pooling?

- **Reduce Financial Burden** — Dramatically reduce perceived funding needed by recognizing all forms of capital, not just money.
- **Unlock Hidden Assets** — Access assets that could never have been bought, like land not for sale but wanting to be part of your vision.
- **Equal Contributions** — Create a process for everyone to bring equal contribution over time, regardless of financial situation.
- **Regenerative Foundations** — Build diverse foundations for a regenerative economic system from the onset.

## How to use this tool (info box)

**Heading:** How to use this tool:

1. Enter your project name and target funding amount
2. Add your immediate contributions (land, money, equipment, etc.)
3. Add your future value commitments (roles you will fill)
4. Download your PDF contribution summary
5. Share the tool link with other contributors to aggregate all contributions
