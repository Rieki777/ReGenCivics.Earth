# Section 1: Series Overview and Mechanics

The Welcome Aboard Quests are how new players root themselves in the Regenerative Renaissance. Ten small, meaningful acts: reflecting, sharing, connecting, pledging, exploring. Each one is designed to help you understand who you are, what you bring, and how you belong to this living movement. They live in the player's profile section (`/profile?tab=quests`) and are also the gateway to making your first Claim in the ReGen Game.

**Where players find it:** A pop-up fires right after they complete their profile, inviting them to "Start Questing!" There is also a persistent link in the main profile area and a "Personal Quests" item in the nav menu.

## How Each Quest is Completed

Every Welcome Aboard Quest is completed by doing two things together:

**Step A: Intellectual and Experiential Value**
Leave a comment on the quest's dedicated forum post. Share your thoughts, experiences, and reflections. Even a single honest sentence is enough. This is the intellectual and experiential value you bring to the ecosystem: your lived experience becoming part of a shared body of knowledge.

**Step B: Social Value**
In your forum comment, include a link to a social media post (any platform) where you share your experience or invite others in. It does not need to be polished. Positive, constructive, or even critical responses are all welcome. This is the social value you bring to the ecosystem: your network becoming part of the wider web of regeneration.

## Q4: Connect with Your Bioregion

**Tagline:** Know where you are. Know what you belong to.

**Reward:** 33 $ReGen + 0.1 RGVoice

**About this quest:**
Most of us live in places we can name but cannot truly read. We know our street address but not our watershed. We know our city but not which migratory birds pass through it each spring. We know our country but not which old-growth trees once covered the land we walk on. This quest is an invitation to research your bioregion, the living ecological community you inhabit, and share what you discover. This kind of rooted knowledge is the foundation of all genuine regenerative action. You cannot tend what you do not know.

**How to complete:**
1. Research your bioregion. Find out what watershed you live in, what native plants and animals call your area home, and one thing about the ecological or land history of where you are. (Try watershedproject.org or inaturalist.org as a starting point.)
2. Share three things you discovered and one thing that surprised you in the forum post below.
3. Post your bioregion discovery on social media to invite others into this kind of rootedness, and include the link in your forum comment.

## Overview of Changes

The Welcome Aboard Quests live in the player's profile section. The current profile page (`client/src/pages/PlayerProfile.tsx`) already has a "Quests" tab. The required changes are:

1. **URL param handling:** The page does not currently read the `?tab=` URL parameter. It always defaults to "overview." Add URL param reading so that `?tab=quests` loads the Quests tab directly.
2. **Rename link target:** Any links pointing to `/profile?tab=contributions` should be updated to point to `/profile?tab=quests`.
3. **New WelcomeAboardQuests component:** A new React component that renders all 10 quest cards with title, tagline, description, reward info, how-to steps, forum link, and a "completed" toggle.
4. **Database state:** Each quest can be tracked via the existing `questsCompleted` JSON array on the player profile. Quest IDs for the Welcome Aboard series: `welcome-aboard-1` through `welcome-aboard-10`.

## 1. Add URL Parameter Handling

**File:** `client/src/pages/PlayerProfile.tsx`

```ts
const searchParams = new URLSearchParams(window.location.search);
const tabParam = searchParams.get("tab") as ProfileTab | null;
const validTabs: ProfileTab[] = ["overview", "quests", "contributions", "settings"];
const [activeTab, setActiveTab] = useState<ProfileTab>(
  tabParam && validTabs.includes(tabParam) ? tabParam : "overview"
);
```

Also update the tab click handler to sync the URL without a page reload:

```ts
onClick={() => {
  setActiveTab(tab.id);
  const url = new URL(window.location.href);
  url.searchParams.set("tab", tab.id);
  window.history.replaceState({}, "", url.toString());
}}
```
