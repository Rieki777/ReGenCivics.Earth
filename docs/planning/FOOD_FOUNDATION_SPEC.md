# Food as Foundation — Site Integration Spec v2
## Updated: 2026-04-01

The thesis to weave through the site: food systems are the physical foundation
of the new economy. Food forests appreciate in land value AND increase in annual
production every year. Better system design means lower ongoing labor and inputs.
The more mature the system, the higher the returns and the less it costs to run.
This makes regenerative land with food systems one of the only asset classes that
both appreciates and compounds output simultaneously. That is the basis for a
stable, growing currency. That is what we are backing.

Writing rules applied throughout: no em-dashes, no contrast framing, no AI-isms,
no rhetorical question openers, no passive inspiration. Direct, grounded, Rye's voice.

Most assets either appreciate or produce income. Rarely both, and rarely

currency is worth. The currency is backed by land, food, and the demonstrated

## PAGE: Land Projects / Incubator (/land-projects or /incubator)

### Change 10 — Add "Why food?" section

Add early on the incubator/land projects page, before the application CTA.

**Section heading:** The food system is the foundation

**Body copy:**
Every land project we work with starts with food. Not because it is
required, but because it is the thing that determines whether a regenerative
community actually works over time.

Food forests front-load the effort. The design, the planting, the first
two or three seasons of tending: that is when you put the most in. After
that, the system starts working for you. Yields increase. Labor decreases.
Inputs drop. A well-designed system from year five onward produces more
food with less effort, every season.

This is why we track it. When you apply to the incubator, we ask about
your food system plan and your current sufficiency levels. Not to gatekeep,
but because this data tells us where you are on the curve. And as your
project matures, those numbers tell the story of what you are building.

crossing over to increasing returns on the right, around the 3-5 year mark.
Single clean SVG, no data labels needed.
