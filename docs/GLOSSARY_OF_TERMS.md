# Glossary of Terms

## Bioregion
Geographic area a player belongs to. Bioregional food economy activity affects Harvest distribution. Food production and community health in your bioregion directly shapes your stake in the system.

## BFF (Bioregional Financing Facility)
Regional funding body that supports local food systems and land projects. BFFs distribute capital and coordination at the bioregional scale, connecting community capital with land project needs.

## Citizenship Tiers
Four-tier system representing a player's commitment level and governance power: Explorer (entry), Co-Creator (earned through quests), Steward (governance participation), Sage (long-game visionary). Each tier has specific requirements, powers, and responsibilities.

## Composting (in game context)
The seasonal decay of scores for inactive players. Keeps the system dynamic and rewards consistent participation. Inactive accounts gradually fade back into lower tiers, returning voting power to active players.

## Contribution Score
Percentile-ranked (0-99) score based on tracked contributions. Determines Harvest share and governance weight. Calculated nightly and visible on your player profile.

## $ReGen
The game token earned through quests and contributions. Backed by food production across the network. $ReGen is the currency of the regenerative economy itself.

## $RCivics
The fund token. Anchored in the investment and capital layer. $RCivics represents ownership and voice in the fund that backs the game.

## RCVoice
Governance voice for the Fund (anchored in $RCivics). RCVoice holders vote on fund allocation, strategy, and which land projects receive investment.

## RGVoice
Governance voice for the Game (anchored in $ReGen). RGVoice holders vote on game mechanics, seasonal protocols, economic parameters, and community direction.

## Food Forest
A designed ecosystem of food-producing trees, shrubs, and plants that increases in output while decreasing in labor over time. The asset base of the $ReGen economy. Food forests are the living foundation of regenerative bioregions.

## Gratitude
Peer-to-peer tokens sent each lunar cycle to recognize contributions that tracking systems miss. Direct recognition from one player to another, outside the formal scoring system.

## Harvest
Seasonal distribution of new $ReGen tokens to contributors, bioregions, organisations, and treasury. Happens four times per year, following seasonal rhythms.

## LocalScale
First integrated food network partner for tracking local food economy activity. Connects farm-to-table transactions into the game's contribution scoring system.

## Proposals
Governance mechanism. Stewards and Sages create proposals that go to trust-weighted vote on Hypha. Proposals shape everything from token emission to seasonal mechanics to land project funding.

## Quest
A real-world task players complete to earn contribution points. Ranges from simple (write about your bioregion) to epic (run a seasonal council). Quests embed regenerative action into the game.

## Rites of Passage
Quests 0-13 that form the player's journey through the game. The structured onboarding sequence every new player moves through.

## Seasonal Council
Governance body of Stewards and Sages that convenes each season. Makes decisions on seasonal protocols, composts inactive players, reviews proposals, and shapes the direction of the game.

## Trust Score
Composite score from endorsements, account age, quests, gratitude received, contribution percentile. Used for governance weight in voting. Higher trust scores give more voting power.

## game_variables
The admin-configurable parameter table. All tunable game numbers live here. Quest rewards, tier thresholds, harvest percentages, and composting rates are all stored as variables, not hardcoded.
