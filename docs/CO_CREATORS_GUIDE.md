# Co-Creators Guide

**Draft for Rye's review. Edit directly in this file, then ask Claude to update the site.**

This guide is for people who want to help build ReGen Civics. If you want to design quests, write forum content, facilitate sessions, or shape the game itself, this is your starting point.

---

## What a Co-Creator Does

Co-creators design quests, facilitate sessions, write the forum content that seeds real participation, and build the game mechanics that make all of it work. The quests, the stories, the community infrastructure, the documentation: co-creators are the ones doing that work.

Co-creation is the deepest form of participation in ReGen Civics. It earns the highest token rewards and the most voice in how the game evolves.

---

## The Four Ways to Co-Create

### 1. Design a Quest

Propose a new quest for the seasonal library or the Epic Quests collection. A good quest proposal includes:

- The title and which season it belongs to (or Anytime)
- The deliverable: what the player actually makes or does
- Why this quest belongs in the regenerative arc
- A suggested $ReGen reward amount (reference the existing reward table)
- One or two example completions (what it looks like when someone does this well)

Submit via the "Dream Up a Quest" option in your Welcome Aboard quests. If your quest gets implemented, you earn 1,111 $ReGen.

---

### 2. Facilitate a Session

ReGen Civics runs regular community sessions: open calls, deep dives, land project showcases, and game design conversations. If you want to host, facilitate, or guest speak, reach out through the Schedule page or the forum.

What we look for in a facilitator:

- Some experience running groups, even informally
- A topic or skill connected to the regenerative arc
- Willingness to be recorded (recordings become part of our library)

Facilitators earn 333 $ReGen per session and an RGVoice token for each session they lead.

---

### 3. Write Forum Seed Content

Each quest thread, season post, and community category needs a strong first comment. Something that shows people what honest, specific sharing looks like in practice. If you write a seed comment that gets placed in the forum, you earn 111 $ReGen per post.

The best seed content comes from someone who has actually done the quest and written something real about it. Reach out to Rye directly with the subject line "Seed comment for [Quest Name]," or post it in the relevant forum thread.

---

### 4. Contribute to Game Design

The game mechanics, token structure, governance model, and quest arc are still being built. The people shaping them are the ones showing up to the design conversations.

Game design contributions include:

- Proposals for new mechanics (how tokens are earned, how governance works, new quest categories)
- Documentation of existing systems, written so new people can actually understand them
- Feedback from direct experience on what's working and what isn't
- Mapping the gap between what we have and what we need

Significant game design contributions are rewarded by admin grant from the token ledger, based on scope and impact.

## How Voice Tokens Work for Co-Creators

RGVoice is earned through facilitation and sustained contribution. It gives you weight in decisions about how the game evolves: which quests get added, how the seasonal arc develops, how $ReGen utility gets designed.

You earn RGVoice by:

- Completing the full Rites of Passage arc: 1 RGVoice per rite at completion
- Completing the Welcome Aboard series: 1 RGVoice for the full 10-quest set
- Facilitating a session: 1 RGVoice per session led
- Sustained co-creation recognized by admin: additional voice grants

Voice governance is still being built. The current form is community calls and forum threads. As the game matures, RGVoice will connect to on-chain governance mechanisms.

## Who This Is For

Co-creators tend to be people who have done at least a few of the Rites of Passage quests and felt there was more they wanted to add. They have a skill or area of knowledge that would make a good quest or session, and time to contribute beyond personal practice. Experience in community building, facilitation, land projects, or regenerative finance is useful. Formal credentials aren't.

---

## Where to Start

1. Complete at least Quest 0 (Fire) and one Rite of Passage. You need your own experience before you can design it for others.

2. Show up to a community session. Watch how they run. Post in the forum about what you noticed.

3. Propose one small thing: a seed comment, a quest idea, a topic for a future session. See how it lands.

4. Connect with Rye directly if you want to go deeper. The community is small enough that there's no gate.
