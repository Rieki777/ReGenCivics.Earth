## Open, not routed to a lane, and why

- **QA2-04's policy half: may a warning badge deny `ballot.vote` at all?** This is a founder ruling,
  not a lane's. It is on his list. **`ballot.vote` is deliberately non-transferable, so a village can
  never take that power back**, which is what makes the question sharp.
- **QA2-04's second half: the warning badge is invisible on the member's own badge surface** (control:
  a granted badge on the same member renders). Not yet routed. It sits on a surface no current lane
  holds, and it should go to whichever lane is nearest when one frees up.
- **Which gratitude cap wins.** Founder's, and CYCLE is explicitly forbidden from choosing.
- **`village-voice`'s minting rule and whether `admin_mint_cycle_cap` should be per token.** Founder's;
  MINT gives a recommendation only.
- **`GET /api/investor-summary` is public and echoes the whole admin-authored document verbatim**, so
  pasting a vault URL into `cta_url` publishes it. Found by INVESTOR while sweeping for other doors.
  A conditional leak rather than an open one. Needs a decision on whether the field should be
  validated or the route narrowed.
- **Vault filename entropy comes from `Math.random()`, not a CSPRNG.** The unguessable-URL posture
  rests on it. INVESTOR raised it rather than fixing it, correctly: the per-document choice now
  carries the weight instead.
