## 1 · The one thing that changed the shape of the product

**R81, and the three rulings that finish it.** After a village launches, **the admin can no longer
create value.** Read R81, R84, R85 and R86 in `INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md` §8 before briefing anything
that touches tokens, governance or launch.

The short version, in the order it actually works:

1. **Before launch, nothing issues at all** (R67). The founder builds the whole Game alone.
2. **Launch is a proposal at 100% unity and 100% quorum, with at least three members** (R74). It is
   the moment issuance turns on, and it is irreversible.
3. **After launch every setting is a village rule** (R84). The admin section becomes readable by
   every member and becomes a **proposal composer**: somebody stages a batch of changes and submits
   the whole list as ONE proposal, which applies automatically if it carries.
4. **Named founders keep a back door until a second handover event** (R85), after which it is gone.
   **This is what stops a village being stranded** by a member who never votes.

**The open question that blocks R84 from being briefed:** the founder said a village may **ratify,
improve, or reject**. Ratify and reject exist. **Improve does not** - a ballot carries or fails and
cannot be amended. He has been asked which of three shapes he wants and **has not answered yet.**
The coordinator's recommendation is on record: **send back to draft with the objections attached**,
because the objection panel already exists and it needs no new voting mechanics.

## 4 · The live defect, if GUARDS has not landed

**`cta_url` accepts any scheme.** Round 7's HONEST lane built a validation that refuses a
call-to-action pointing into the vault, and it is good, but **it never looks at the scheme**. A
`javascript:` URL parses, is not under `/api/uploads/`, and saves. **`GET /api/visit-config` and
`GET /api/investor-summary` are both public and echo the whole document back.** R82 item 7 rules the
fix: an allowlist of http, https, mailto and same-origin relative paths.
